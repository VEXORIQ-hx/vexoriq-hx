GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I'm Ahmad Shayan, a web development enthusiast based in Lucknow, focused on building responsive, user-friendly websites that bring ideas to life.

I work primarily with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy the challenge of turning a concept or design into a clean, functional interface. I care about the details — layout, spacing, interaction, and how everything works together to create a coherent experience.

Currently pursuing my BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) at Integral University, Lucknow. Outside of coursework, I spend my time building personal projects, learning new frontend techniques, and sharpening my understanding of what makes a website genuinely good.
